# THE LAUNCH DOSSIER — decision memo

## Verdict
CONDITIONAL GO — Cadence is launch-viable as a real company, but only as a Pinch-channel business with a written partnership, not as a standalone direct-sales venture. The unit economics (~94% GM, break-even at 15-30 merchants) and the 12-24 month competitive window are real; the venture-scale question stays open until the pilot proves >=10 points of attributable recovery uplift over incumbent auto-rebill (Ezypay-class already recovers >50%). Launch it — but the launch IS the Pinch partnership plus one honest pilot, executed while salaried at WeMoney. Two hard gates before Demo Night: written WeMoney IP carve-out + Pty Ltd holding the IP, and the understanding that Pinch's DDRSA must bless re-presentation and payer-agreed date variation or the core loop is non-compliant on its own rail.

**Conviction: medium**

## Reasons FOR

- Near-indestructible unit economics: ~$5.25 revenue per $35 recovery vs ~$0.30 COGS (2-3 SMS, nil compute) = ~94% gross margin; break-even at 15-30 merchants; the model survives a full 50% haircut on recovery uplift. Success-only pricing (15% of recovered, native applicationFee, $0 on healthy volume) is also a wedge incumbents structurally cannot copy — Ezypay/Xplor/Debitsuccess monetise failure via $21.90-$29.90 payer dishonour fees, so matching Cadence cannibalises their own revenue (innovator's dilemma moat).
- Clean legal runway with a defensible mechanic: operates today without an AFSL as a pure merchant-agent software layer (no funds handling, no payer fees, no advice; ASIC low-value NCPF relief runs to April 2031), PSRA realistically doesn't bite before ~2028 with a sub-$8m/month exemption and the Pinch/Fiserv authorised-arrangement route as the cheap endgame. The consent SMS is not just UX — payer-agreed variation takes effect immediately under standard DDR terms, so the SMS is the legal instrument that converts a re-timed debit into an authorised one, and the stored consent receipt is the defence against BECS's no-questions-asked 7-year dispute regime.
- The channel and the capital are literally in the judging room, and the gap is real: Pinch has no recovery product (the exact gap GoCardless filled with Success+), earns ~1% processing on every dollar Cadence recovers, runs a live partner program and co-announcement playbook to piggyback, and sits inside Fiserv (US$365m acquisition, earn-out-incentivised founders) — which is both distribution upside and the exit floor. Competitors are channel-mismatched for Pinch's Xero-native SMB base: GoCardless is enterprise, Stripe explicitly excludes BECS from Smart Retries (2-attempt cap), giving a 12-24 month practical window.

## Reasons AGAINST

- The beachhead stat is inflated 2-3x and the incumbent bar is high: the '15% naive decline' is a manual-billing artefact (EFM pre-Ezypay); electronically-billed gyms run 3-5% first-attempt failure, Ezypay already auto-rebills >50% of failures and GoCardless Success+ recovers ~70% at 0.5-1% pricing (15-30x cheaper than Cadence's 15%). The attributable uplift Cadence must prove is far narrower than the pitch implies — below ~10 points over the merchant's existing auto-rebill, Cadence is a feature, not a company.
- Total structural dependency on Pinch: the DDRSA must authorise re-presentation and electronic payer-agreed variation (merchants use Pinch's templates — no blessing, no compliant loop); the zero-CAC first-5 motion is Pinch's merchant book and warm intros; and the binding 18-24 month threat is Fiserv building recovery in-house. One counterparty controls the rail, the channel, and the competitive clock simultaneously.
- The ceiling and the founder's position are both constrained: per-site revenue is $500-$4,000/yr, SAM ~$10-30M/yr fee revenue across all three verticals, franchise gym chains are contractually closed (Debitsuccess/Xplor exclusive dealing, Hapana on Stripe), leaving ~3,000 Xero-billing independents; a solo founder working full-time at WeMoney faces the ~17%-of-VC-closers solo discount, an unresolved IP-assignment/moonlighting clause, and a 6-9 month channel lag before Xero App Store and bookkeeper channels compound. Venture scale exists only via Fiserv/Clover distribution or geo expansion — neither is in his control.

## The 90-day post-win plan

GATES BEFORE DEMO NIGHT (Week 0): (1) written WeMoney IP carve-out + moonlighting consent — without a clean IP chain both the company and the Fiserv path collapse; (2) incorporate a Pty Ltd and assign the IP into it; (3) register the ACMA SMS Sender ID (deadline already passed 30 June 2026 — an unregistered alphanumeric ID renders every consent SMS 'Unverified' and scam-adjacent, gutting the trust mechanic). WEEKS 1-2 (post-Demo-Night, while hot): the Pinch conversation with three specific written asks — (a) DDRSA amendment authorising re-presentation after dishonour and payer-agreed debit-date variation via electronic confirmation (the hard dependency: resolve in writing or the core loop is non-compliant), (b) sandbox + dishonour-data access to Pinch's ledger, (c) a Managing/Referral Partner slot with co-marketing and 3-5 named warm intros from their service-SMB book. Sign nothing exclusive; signal zero acquihire interest. WEEKS 2-4 (compliance hardening): build the consent-receipt spec as the core data model (payer's exact words, timestamp, linked debit ID, stored in payment metadata — this is the legal foundation, not a feature); lock SMS templates as strictly factual DCEM under the Spam Act (sender ID, no promotional drift); adopt RG 96 contact-hour/frequency norms; draft APP-grade privacy posture + automated-decision-making disclosure language (obligation commences 10 Dec 2026 and ML-decided debit timing is squarely in scope). WEEKS 3-6 (pilot recruitment): 2-3 merchants from Pinch's EXISTING service-SMB book (the product must be vertical-agnostic at launch — first customers will not be gyms), each with 150-200+ active mandates; pull a 6-cycle historical baseline from Pinch's ledger; pre-register the experiment design — payday-heuristic comparator arm, attributable-uplift metric vs the merchant's existing auto-rebill outcome, case-study rights traded for the free period. WEEKS 6-12 (run and measure): 2+ live debit cycles; the decision metric is >=10 points of ATTRIBUTABLE recovery uplift over incumbent auto-rebill on >=3 months of real Pinch debit data. WEEKS 10-13 (fork): metric hit → publish the case study, trigger Pinch's co-announcement press template, open the Xero App Store certification track (needs 5-10 active customers first; budget its 15% rev-share), approach Archangel Ventures ($100k-$500k, fintech-scoped, no residency); metric missed → Cadence is a Pinch/Fiserv feature — convert the relationship into a build-with/earn-out conversation while it is still warm. EMPLOYMENT RULING: do NOT quit WeMoney in these 90 days under any scenario. The $50k prize is 6-9 months frugal runway, not a quit event. The full-time trigger is ~15-25 signed merchants / ~$5-8k MRR with the Pinch agreement in writing — realistically month 6-9 at the earliest. Antler ($225k/12%, full-time residency) and Startmate ($120k/8%, full-time) are structurally off the table until then.

## The money model

PRICING: 15% of Cadence-ATTRIBUTED recovered dollars only, charged as a native Pinch applicationFee; $0 on healthy volume; never charge on all cleared payments (attribution discipline is the pricing-power defence — Forrester puts manual recovery cost at 11-15% of failed value, collections at 20-30%, so 15% is defensible in SMB but only on clearly attributed saves; GoCardless bundles the same outcome at 0.5-1% in enterprise, so SMB is the only segment with pricing power). UNIT ECONOMICS: $35 average debit → $5.25 revenue per recovery; COGS ~$0.30 (2-3 Twilio SMS at ~A$0.08 + nil compute) = ~94% gross margin. Per-merchant revenue $500-$4,000/yr (300-member gym ~$3-4k, 500-member ~$5-7k). Pinch earns ~1% processing on every recovered dollar — the partner's incentive is built into the mechanic. BREAK-EVEN: ~15-30 merchants covers frugal solo run-rate; the A$50k prize ≈ 6-9 months runway. YEAR 1 (moonlighting, sized honestly on 4-8% first-attempt failure, NOT the 15% manual-billing artefact): 10-25 merchants, mostly Pinch's existing service SMBs → A$15-60k ARR; the year-1 objective is the >=10pt-uplift case study, not revenue. YEAR 2: base case 60-120 sites via bookkeeper/Xero channels (which only compound after 5-10 customers + a published case study — plan for the 6-9 month lag) → A$100-300k ARR; upside case = one franchise-HQ or vertical-SaaS deal (Anytime Fitness HQ-scale = A$580k-$1.2M ARR at $1-2k/site) plus the childcare expansion — the larger, legally-tailwinded pool ($8-12B/yr gap fees, electronic collection mandated since July 2023, 15,300 CCS services); fitness is the wedge, childcare justifies the company. SENSITIVITY: at half the modelled uplift the business still clears 94% GM but the founder-full-time threshold doubles to 300-500 sites — channel/HQ deals become mandatory, not optional; direct SMB sales alone can never reach full-time (~A$250k ARR). CEILING: SAM ~A$10-30M/yr fee revenue; 3-yr SOM A$0.5-2M ARR — a strong solo/seed business; venture scale requires Fiserv/Clover distribution (the judges ARE that path) or expansion beyond AU.

## The three killers

- KILLER 1 — Pinch says no, or builds it themselves. The DDRSA blessing (re-presentation + electronic payer-agreed variation) is a hard compliance dependency, the merchant book is the entire zero-CAC GTM, and Fiserv building recovery in-house at 18-24 months is the binding competitive threat (GoCardless already ships Success+ native). MITIGATION: convert hackathon heat into a written commercial agreement within 30 days of Demo Night — DDRSA amendment, data access, co-marketing — structured so Pinch wins on every save (~1% processing on recovered volume; earn-out-incentivised founders benefit directly); operate inside their existing Managing/Referral Partner template so the posture is partner-not-vendor; and if Fiserv signals intent to internalise, that IS the exit path — negotiate build-with/earn-out from live recovered-dollar data, not from a demo.
- KILLER 2 — the attributable uplift isn't there. Electronically-billed merchants already run 3-5% failure and Ezypay-class auto-rebill already recovers >50%; GoCardless proves 70% is reachable. If Cadence cannot show >=10 points of uplift OVER the incumbent auto-rebill outcome, it is a Pinch feature priced 15-30x above the GoCardless benchmark — and a pilot that only shows 'retrying helps' converts nobody. MITIGATION: pre-registered pilot design with a payday-heuristic comparator arm and a 6-cycle historical baseline from Pinch's ledger; size all projections on 4-8% failure; differentiate on what blind retries cannot do — consent-based reschedule-to-the-funded-day, evidence-grade receipts, zero payer dishonour fees — and measure member-churn saves (the gym's real P&L pain) alongside recovery rate, because a saved membership is worth far more than a saved debit.
- KILLER 3 — a compliance strike destroys the trust mechanic. BECS disputes are honoured no-questions-asked for up to 7 years: one mis-timed debit without a recorded consent receipt is an automatic Valid Claim and a strike against the merchant's and Pinch's standing; an unregistered sender ID gets every SMS labelled 'Unverified' next to scam messages (deadline already passed 30 June 2026); any promotional drift in the SMS voids the Spam Act DCEM exemption (ACMA actively prosecutes mischaracterised messages); and the 10 Dec 2026 automated-decision-making disclosure obligation squarely covers an ML model deciding when consumers are debited. MITIGATION: the consent receipt (exact payer words, timestamp, linked debit, stored in payment metadata) is the product's core data model and legal foundation — build it first, not as a feature; register the ACMA sender ID before the first pilot message; keep every SMS strictly factual with RG 96 contact-hour/frequency norms; ship ADM-disclosure and APP-grade privacy language now, ahead of Privacy Act Tranche 2.

## How this changes the pitch

- Kill the 'smart retries for Australia' framing entirely — Carolyn Breeze launched GoCardless in Australia and knows Success+ already runs ML retry timing on BECS AUD; a novelty pitch dies on sight. Reframe as: consent-based RESCHEDULE-to-the-payer's-funded-day on the merchant's EXISTING PayFac account, where the payer's SMS confirmation is the legal mechanism (payer-agreed variation takes effect immediately under DDR terms) and the stored consent receipt is the dispute defence — three things Success+ does not do.
- Lead with the corrected market math before a judge corrects it for you: electronically-billed failure is 4-8% (the 15% figure is EFM's pre-electronic manual-billing artefact), the incumbent bar is Ezypay's >50% auto-rebill and GoCardless's 70%, and the committed pilot metric is >=10 points of ATTRIBUTABLE uplift over the merchant's existing recovery on real Pinch ledger data with a payday-heuristic comparator. Knowing the honest numbers is the credibility signal that separates a founder from a hackathon team.
- 'We will actually launch this' converts from claim to evidence: Pty Ltd formed and holding the IP, ACMA sender ID registered, consent-receipt architecture built into the demo, pilot design pre-registered with a named kill-metric, and a 90-day plan with an explicit quit-trigger (~15-25 merchants / $5-8k MRR). Judges scoring 'commercial potential' and weighing 'ongoing opportunities within the Pinch/TFU ecosystem' are pricing exactly one question — will this person still be shipping in 90 days — and this answers it with artifacts, not enthusiasm.
- Make the three Pinch asks explicit in the close: (1) DDRSA language authorising re-presentation and electronic payer-agreed date variation, (2) dishonour-data/sandbox access for the pilot baseline, (3) a partner-program slot with a co-announcement (their existing Annature press-release playbook). Asking for specific, cheap, revenue-positive things repositions Demo Night from 'judge my project' to 'first commercial meeting with my channel partner.'
- Show Pinch and Fiserv their own win explicitly: every recovered debit earns Pinch ~1% processing it currently loses; Cadence closes Pinch's one competitive gap against GoCardless Advanced (which bundles Success+ at 0.75%+5c) at zero build cost; and a new 300-member gym is ~$4.5k/yr of processing to Pinch — Cadence is a merchant-acquisition story for them, not just a feature.
- Weaponise success-only pricing as the structurally uncopyable wedge: incumbents (Ezidebit $21.90-$29.90 payer dishonour fees, Debitsuccess since 1994) MONETISE failure — copying Cadence's model means cannibalising their own fee revenue. Pinch charges merchants $5 per decline and its judges get to own the 'we monetise success, they monetise failure' story in their own ecosystem.

## Dual-path ruling (company vs join-Fiserv)

Run both paths simultaneously under ONE posture — 'commercial partner on Pinch rails' — and let a single metric fork them. The paths are convergent, not conflicting, until pilot data exists: everything that makes Cadence valuable as a company (written Pinch partnership, evidence-grade consent receipts, attributable uplift on live Pinch debits, real merchants) is exactly what makes the founder valuable to Fiserv. So the ruling is: build the company artifacts, and the join-Fiserv option prices itself off them. FIVE RULES: (1) Zero acquihire signalling before live recovered-dollar data — expressing eagerness to be absorbed zeroes leverage on BOTH paths; if Pinch/Fiserv raise it first, the answer is 'let's see what the pilot shows.' (2) Defer the Scalare cheque until Fiserv's intent is known — a ~$100-250k micro-cap cheque with services strings, from a CEO who sits next to the acquirer channel, muddies a later Fiserv negotiation and buys nothing the prize money doesn't; if external capital is needed post-pilot, Archangel Ventures ($100k-$500k, fintech-scoped, no residency) is the only fit — Antler and Startmate require quitting WeMoney and are off the table. (3) The pilot metric is the fork: >=10 points attributable uplift → company path leads (case study, Pinch co-announcement, Xero channel, Archangel), with Fiserv corp-dev as the natural series-A alternative once recovered-volume data compounds; <10 points → Cadence is honestly a Pinch/Fiserv feature — convert immediately to a build-with/earn-out or senior-role conversation while the hackathon relationship is warm, which is a good outcome, not a failure (Fiserv paid US$365m for Pinch with ~$27m earn-outs; they pay for proven mechanics and the people who built them). (4) Keep the IP chain surgically clean — WeMoney written carve-out before Demo Night, Pty Ltd owns everything — because a clouded chain kills the acquisition path even faster than the venture path. (5) Never grant Pinch exclusivity without a paid revenue commitment: exclusivity is the one ask that quietly executes the acquihire (kills the company's option value) while paying nothing for it — the partnership agreement should trade co-marketing and data access for case-study rights and revenue share, never for exclusivity.
